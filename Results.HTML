Analysis:

As the experiment was held with the aim to test which material is the best heat insulator, we can conclude that the results are directly related to the heat resistance of the cup material.

Since the amount and temperature of the water were controlled variables, we know that the amount of cooling time differed based on how well each material resists heat loss.

‘Heat resistance’ is the material’s ability to maintain a high temperature. This means that the material with the worse heat resistance would have taken the least amount of time for the water to cool down to 50oC.

Our results display that the plastic cup took the least amount of time for the water to reach 50oC, with 6 minutes and 26 seconds which means that it has the best thermal conductivity(heat travels the most easily through the material).

However, the porcelain cup took 16 minutes and 37 seconds, which is the longest amount of time it took for the water to cool down. This indicates that porcelain is the best heat insulator.

Conclusion:

Through our experiment, we came to a conclusion that porcelain is the most effective thermal insulator.

The results of our experiment support our hypothesis, which was that ‘The porcelain cup will be the best heat insulator when the cups(paper, plastic, porcelain cup, stainless steel, glass) are compared based on the time it takes for the contained water to go from 60˚C to 50˚C.

’After conducting the experiment, our group had an idea for another experiment. As the results displayed that porcelain was the most effective thermal insulator, we thought of conducting an experiment where we pour water of different temperatures into many porcelain cups, and testing how long each one takes to drop 10oC from the original temperature. This can help us explore more into the aspects of thermal conduction, and insulation.
