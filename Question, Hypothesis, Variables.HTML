Research Question & Hypothesis:

Research Question
What type of cup will keep 40ml of water that is ~60°C until it drops to 50°C for the longest time when tested without a lid in the classroom? (paper, plastic, porcelain, stainless steel(double wall), or glass)
Hypothesis
The porcelain cup will be the best heat insulator when the cups (paper, plastic, porcelain cup, stainless steel, glass) are compared based on the time it takes for the contained water to go from 60°C to 50°C.

Variables:

Variables
Independent
  • The material of the cup
Dependent
  • The time it takes for the contained water to drop from 60°C to 50°C (min & sec)
Control
  • Volume of liquid (40ml)
  • Original temperature of liquid (60°C)
  • Starting time of measurement
  • External temperature (classroom environment)
