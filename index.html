Abstract:

This experiment tested which type of cup material out of plastic, porcelain, glass, paper and stainless steel has the highest heat resistance.

The purpose was to learn more about thermal conduction, and determine which cup will provide the best heat insulation for hot liquids, as we can use the information in real life.

Our hypothesis was ‘The Porcelain cup will be the best heat insulator when the cups (paper, plastic, porcelain cup, stainless steel, glass) are compared based on the time it takes for the contained water to go from 60 degrees to 50 degrees’.

The temperature of the water for all materials were all the same(60oC), and we measured the time until the water temperature dropped 10oC.

The result supported our hypothesis since it showed that porcelain is the best insulator because it took 16 minutes and 37 seconds to cool down 10 degrees which is the longest time among all the materials to cool down to 50˚C.
